### 💫 About Me

I am a passionate **Software Engineer** specializing in **AI Systems Infrastructure**, **Deep Learning Acceleration**, and **Modern Full-Stack Applications**. My work spans low-level kernel benchmarking & custom sparse attention mechanisms to high-throughput web architectures and algorithmic design.

- 🔬 **Currently Researching & Building**: Microbenchmarking sparse attention backends (**RSA-X**) in PyTorch/Triton, hardware-aware latency/throughput profiling.
- ⚡ **Core Strengths**: Custom Deep Learning Engines from Scratch, System Optimization (C++/PyTorch/Triton), RESTful Architectures, and Responsive Web Platforms.
- 🎯 **Career Goal**: Engineering high-efficiency AI model backends & scalable full-stack platforms that turn complex research into production-grade software.
- 💬 **Ask me about**: Transformer architectures, attention density profiling, NumPy autograd engines, C++ concurrency, React/Next.js full-stack development.
